applies:
  - who: You draw a government pension through a bank
    status: act
    text: File your life certificate between 1 and 30 November. The government's own words are that it's needed "for continuation of pension".[^1][^4]
  - who: You're 80 or older
    status: check
    text: Your window may already be open. In past years pensioners over 80 could file from 1 October. Filing early costs you nothing.
  - who: You're helping a parent
    status: act
    text: You can run the face app on your own phone as the "operator". The face that gets scanned is your parent's, and the certificate is theirs.[^2]
  - who: Someone calls or messages to "update" your life certificate
    status: act
    text: Hang up. That's the scam doing the rounds right now. Kerala Police say fraudsters are sending APK files over WhatsApp from profiles that carry bank logos.[^5]
rules:
  - quote: 'Department of Pension and Pensioners'' Welfare, 13 August 2025: "Pensioners have to submit Life Certificate every year in the month of November for continuation of pension."'
    plain: It's a yearly job, and November is the month. This year's campaign runs from 1 to 30 November 2026, with camps at banks, post offices and railway offices.[^1][^4]
  - quote: 'Jeevan Pramaan Face App guide: "Any Person can act as an operator."'
    plain: The phone doesn't have to belong to the pensioner. Your son, daughter or neighbor can set it up once and use it for you. Your Aadhaar does have to be registered with the bank or office that pays your pension.[^2]
  - quote: 'India Post Payments Bank: "There will be no doorstep charges levied for IPPB or non-IPPB customers for issuance of DLC."'
    plain: A postman can come home and do it, even if you don't bank with IPPB. You pay Rs 70 only when the certificate is actually generated.[^3]
steps:
  - "Check the phone first. You need Android 7.0 or later, 4 GB of RAM, a 5 MP camera and an internet connection. Install two apps from the Play Store only: AadhaarFaceRD and Jeevan Pramaan Face App.[^2]"
  - "Keep the pensioner's Aadhaar number, mobile number and PPO number handy. Register the operator once with an OTP and a face scan, then enter the pensioner's details, pick the PPO and tap Scan. Sit facing even light, take off dark glasses, and blink when the app asks.[^2]"
  - "Wait for the SMS. It carries a Pramaan ID and a link to download the certificate. Save both, since that ID is your proof that you filed.[^2]"
  - "If the phone route won't work, book a postman through your nearest post office or IPPB's online request form, and keep Rs 70 ready. Your bank branch is the other fallback.[^3]"
  - "If anyone calls, messages or sends an app file about your life certificate, cut the call and ring your bank's official number. If money has already gone, call 1930 or file at cybercrime.gov.in straight away.[^5]"
finePrint:
  - AadhaarFaceRD doesn't put an icon on your home screen. It shows up only under Settings, then Apps, so don't reinstall it thinking it failed.[^2]
  - Each PPO gets its own Pramaan ID. If you draw two pensions, say your own and a family pension, select both PPOs during the scan.[^2]
  - Copy the PPO details exactly from your pension papers. The guide warns that wrong details can get the certificate rejected by the paying bank.[^2]
  - Screen sharing is the newer trick. A retired employee in Hyderabad lost nearly Rs 11.69 lakh after "bank officials" got him to share his screen on a WhatsApp video call, supposedly to update his life certificate.[^6]
unknowns:
  - We couldn't find the 2026 campaign order from the Department of Pension. The 1 to 30 November dates come from railway notices for this year's campaign, DLC 5.0.[^4]
  - Whether the early October window for pensioners over 80 applies again this year. It did in 2025, but we haven't seen it confirmed for 2026.
  - What exactly happens if you miss 30 November. Banks usually hold the pension until you file and then pay the arrears, but we found no official 2026 statement on timing.
  - State government and EPFO pensioners can run on different schedules. If your pension isn't from the central government, check the month with whoever pays it.
take: >-
  Face authentication is one of the better things India's digital ID setup has done for older people. No bank queue, no fingerprint reader that can't read worn-out fingers, and a family member can do the whole thing from their own phone. The catch is that the real process looks a lot like the fraud: install an app, enter an OTP, scan your face. So here's the rule I'd give my own parents. The real one starts with you opening the Play Store. It never starts with someone calling you. No bank needs to phone you to "update" a life certificate, and no genuine process sends an app over WhatsApp. If a parent of yours draws a pension, sit with them this weekend and do it together on your phone. It's a short job, and next November they'll know what the real thing looks like.
