steps:
  - Keep paying by UPI exactly as you do now. Nothing on your side changes.
  - If a shop adds a "UPI charge" to your bill, ask them to take it off. The rule doesn't allow it.
  - If they refuse, keep the bill or a screenshot and raise it through your UPI app's help section or your bank.
  - "Shop owners: check your monthly UPI QR total. Three months in a row above Rs 1 lakh moves you into the paid tier."
finePrint:
  - The small-shop exemption isn't permanent. Cross Rs 1 lakh a month on UPI QR for three straight months and you're reclassified.
  - Credit on UPI, such as a RuPay credit card linked to UPI, sits under separate credit rules. This fee framework doesn't cover it.
  - AutoPay mandates (SIPs, OTT subscriptions, bills on autopay) have no prescribed fee under this framework.
  - 'Education fees get "flat-fee structures or capped processing rates", with no number attached.'
unknowns:
  - The FAQ's cover page says 15 September 2026 while its answers say 15 October. We're going with 15 October, the date in the rules themselves.[^1]
  - How "no passing it on" gets enforced at a busy counter. The FAQ states the rule but names no penalty.
  - The exact fee for school and college payments.
take: >-
  Zero-fee UPI was always being paid for by someone, and a 0.4% charge on big shop payments is a fair way to share the bill. The risk is the counter, not the rule. Expect a few shops to try a "UPI surcharge" on large bills in the first weeks, and push back when they do. In June 2025 the government called talk of a UPI fee "completely false", so a little scepticism about how this rolls out is earned.[^3]
