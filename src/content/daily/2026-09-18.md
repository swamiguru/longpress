    heading: "Incognito mode never made you anonymous"
    body: >-
      Time to kill a myth: Incognito is not anonymous. Private/Incognito mode only stops your own device from saving history, cookies and form data. Your internet provider, your office or college Wi-Fi, every site you open and anything you log into can still see you. What it's actually good for is not saving history on a shared laptop or logging into a second account, and that's about it. For real privacy on public Wi-Fi a trusted VPN helps, but even that is no invisibility cloak. 🕵️
    image: "/images/daily/2026-09-18/card_4.png"
    problem: >-
      Millions assume Incognito/Private mode hides their browsing from everyone, and do sensitive things thinking they're invisible.
    breakthrough: >-
      Incognito only clears local traces on your device (history, cookies, form data); it was never designed to hide you from networks or the sites you visit.
    catch: >-
      Your ISP, network admin, the websites you open and any account you sign into still see your activity, and a VPN hides your network path, not your logged-in identity.
    forYou: >-
      Use Incognito for shared devices and second logins; for real network privacy use a trusted VPN on public Wi-Fi, and never treat either as true anonymity.
