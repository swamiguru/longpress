    body: >-
      Instagram has a built-in filter called Hidden Words that most people never switch on. Go to Settings → How others can interact with you → Hidden Words, turn on 'Hide comments' and 'Hide message requests', then add your own custom keywords. Instagram auto-filters spammy, scammy and abusive comments and DM requests before they ever reach your notifications — no manual blocking required.
    problem: >-
      Public Instagram accounts get buried in spam comments, scam DMs and abuse, and blocking accounts one at a time is a losing game.
    breakthrough: >-
      Hidden Words (Settings → How others can interact with you → Hidden Words) lets you enable Instagram's own offensive-content filters and add custom keyword filters. Flagged comments and message requests are hidden automatically before you see them.
    catch: >-
      It hides rather than deletes, and keyword lists need occasional tuning — spammers adapt, so you'll want to add new terms over time.
    forYou: >-
      Turn it on today, especially if your account is public: enable both toggles and drop in the words and phrases you're tired of seeing.
