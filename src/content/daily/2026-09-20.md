    heading: "No, charging overnight doesn't kill your battery"
    body: >-
      'Charging overnight kills your battery' is one of the stickiest tech myths going. In reality, modern phones stop pulling power once they hit 100%, and Optimized (or Adaptive) Charging deliberately holds the battery at around 80% overnight, topping up to full just before your usual wake time. The things that actually age a lithium battery are heat and repeatedly draining to 0%. So you can stop babysitting the charger: keep the phone cool, avoid running it flat, and turn on Optimized Charging.
    image: "/images/daily/2026-09-20/illustration_4.png"
    problem: >-

    breakthrough: >-
      Modern phones stop drawing power at 100%, and Optimized/Adaptive Charging holds the battery near 80% overnight before topping up just before you wake - so overnight charging isn't the villain.
    catch: >-
      Batteries do still degrade - but the real accelerants are heat and repeated deep discharges to 0%, not the act of charging overnight.
    forYou: >-
      Turn on Optimized/Adaptive Charging, keep the phone out of hot spots (no charging under a pillow), and avoid regularly hitting 0% - that's the whole battery-care checklist.
