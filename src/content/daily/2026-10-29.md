    heading: "Check how many SIMs are registered on your Aadhaar"
    body: >-
      To see how many mobile connections are registered on your Aadhaar, open Sanchar Saathi, choose Know Your Mobile Connections, enter your mobile number and the OTP, and read the list, as [BankBazaar](https://www.bankbazaar.com/banks/how-to-report-unauthorise-sim-cards-through-tafcop.html) walks through. Anything you don't recognise can be flagged as Not my number or Not required. It takes two minutes, and it's the one scam defence you can run before anything has gone wrong.
    problem: >-
      SIM fraud runs on other people's IDs. Someone gets a connection issued on your Aadhaar, and your name sits behind the number making the scam calls.
    breakthrough: >-
      Sanchar Saathi, the Department of Telecommunications' portal and app, lets you list every mobile number registered on your Aadhaar. You enter your mobile number, confirm the OTP, scan the list, and mark anything unfamiliar as Not my number or Not required. Submitting gives you a reference ID, per [BankBazaar](https://www.bankbazaar.com/banks/how-to-report-unauthorise-sim-cards-through-tafcop.html).
    catch: >-
      It shows what's on record, not who is using the number, and the on-screen labels can change, so go by what the portal says. Fake apps with similar names exist on the Play Store, so check the developer is the DoT before you install.
    forYou: >-
      Do it today, and again after you lose a document or change address. Never read an OTP out to anyone who calls you, whatever badge they claim.
    question: "How do I check how many SIM cards are registered in my name in India?"
    answer: >-
      Open the Sanchar Saathi app or portal, choose Know Your Mobile Connections, enter your mobile number and the OTP, and it lists the numbers registered on your Aadhaar. Mark any you don't recognise as Not my number or Not required and submit. You get a reference ID.
    qa:
      - q: "Is the Sanchar Saathi app official, and are there fake versions?"
        a: "Sanchar Saathi is run by the Department of Telecommunications, Government of India. Several fake apps with similar names exist on the Google Play Store, so confirm the developer is the DoT before downloading, and never read your OTP out to anyone who calls you, even if they sound official."
      - q: "What should I do if a SIM I did not buy shows up on my Aadhaar?"
        a: "Select that number in the list, choose This is not my number or Not required, and submit the report. You get a reference ID to track the request. If money was already lost through that number, tell your bank and the police cyber cell as well, because the SIM check alone does not recover funds."
