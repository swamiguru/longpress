    heading: "Stop getting generic AI answers — make it interview you first"
    body: >-
      Most weak AI answers come from weak prompts: you ask a broad question, the model guesses what you meant, and you get mush. Fix it by flipping who asks the questions. Paste this before your real request: 'Before you answer, ask me up to 5 questions, one at a time, to fully understand what I need. Wait for each answer before asking the next. Once you have enough, give me your best response.' Suddenly the model pulls out your context, constraints and goals instead of assuming them — and the final answer is sharper, more specific and actually usable. Works in ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude.
    problem: >-
      People fire a one-line prompt at an AI, it fills the gaps with assumptions, and the result is generic — so they blame the model when the real issue is missing context.
    breakthrough: >-
      Flip the direction of questions with a copy-paste line: 'Before you answer, ask me up to 5 questions, one at a time, to fully understand what I need. Wait for each answer before asking the next. Then give me your best response.' The model gathers your specifics before committing to an answer. Works across ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude.
    catch: >-
      It adds a few back-and-forth steps, so it is overkill for simple lookups — use it for meaty tasks like planning, writing and decisions, not for what is the capital of X. And you still have to answer its questions honestly for it to help.
    forYou: >-
      Save this prompt as a text-replacement shortcut and paste it before any important request today — you trade 30 seconds of questions for a dramatically better answer.
