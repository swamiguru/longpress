    heading: "UPI merchant fee gets a Diwali reprieve, not a cancellation"
    body: >-
      NPCI has deferred the UPI merchant discount rate, which was set to start on October 15, until after Diwali, Inc42 reported on October 13. The proposed fee is 0.4% on select shop payments above Rs 2,000, capped at Rs 300. Calling it a festive-season breather is fair. Calling it a U-turn isn't, because the committee says a new date will be notified, and merchants should plan for it rather than hope it quietly goes away.
    image: "/images/daily/2026-10-14/illustration_2.png"
    problem: >-
      UPI has run on zero merchant fees for years, which made it universal but left banks and payment apps footing the bill for the rails.
    breakthrough: >-
      NPCI's UPI and Services Steering Committee pushed the MDR on eligible P2M payments past Diwali, keeping the festive season in mind, per [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/575789/). The rate stays at 0.4% above Rs 2,000 with a Rs 300 cap, and the new start date will be announced separately.
    catch: >-
      There's no date yet, and Inc42 says it isn't the January 1, 2027 date floated earlier, so merchants are planning blind.
    forYou: >-
      As a customer, nothing changes and paying friends stays free. If you run a shop, use the extra weeks to check whether your turnover falls under any exemption, and don't start adding UPI surcharges at the counter.
    question: "Is the UPI MDR charge starting on October 15, 2026?"
    answer: >-
      No. On October 13, 2026, NPCI's UPI steering committee deferred the merchant discount rate on select shop payments, due October 15, until after Diwali. A new date will be notified. Person-to-person UPI payments stay free, so this mainly affects merchants.
    qa:
      - q: "How much is the proposed UPI MDR on merchant payments?"
        a: "The plan was a 0.4% merchant discount rate on eligible person-to-merchant UPI payments above Rs 2,000, capped at Rs 300 per transaction, according to Inc42. The charge falls on the merchant's side. The committee was also reportedly weighing an exemption for businesses with turnover up to Rs 40 lakh a year."
      - q: "Will customers pay extra for UPI payments at shops after Diwali?"
        a: "The proposed MDR is a charge on the merchant's side of the transaction, not a customer fee, so a counter surcharge isn't part of NPCI's plan. If a shop adds one, ask why. Payments to friends and family stay free regardless of the deferral, and no new date has been set yet."

    body: >-
      Spotify lets you exclude any playlist or track from your taste profile: open it, tap the three dots and pick Exclude from your taste profile. This is the fix for the shared kids' playlist, the sleep-sounds loop or the workout mix your partner plays on your account. It takes up to 48 hours to settle, and those streams drop out of Wrapped too, which some of us will appreciate.
    image: "/images/daily/2026-10-14/illustration_3.png"
    problem: >-
      One heavily played playlist that isn't really your taste, like nursery rhymes, white noise or a party mix, can skew Discover Weekly and Daily Mixes for weeks.
    breakthrough: >-
      Spotify's [support page](https://support.spotify.com/ly-en/article/exclude-playlists-or-tracks-from-your-taste-profile/) describes the option: playlist or track, three-dot menu, Exclude from your taste profile, on iPhone, Android and desktop.
    catch: >-
      It only works for playlists and tracks, not artists or albums, and the same songs played from Liked Songs still count.
    forYou: >-
      Exclude the two or three playlists other people play on your account, then give it a week before judging your recommendations.
    question: "How do I stop a playlist from affecting my Spotify recommendations?"
    answer: >-
      Open the playlist in Spotify, tap the three-dot menu and choose Exclude from your taste profile. It also works on single tracks. Spotify says changes take up to 48 hours, and Discover Weekly may only update the following week.
    qa:
      - q: "Does excluding a Spotify playlist from my taste profile affect Wrapped?"
        a: "Yes. Spotify's support page says streams from excluded playlists or tracks are left out of Wrapped data points such as top artists and top songs, though your total listening time still counts. So the kids' playlist or sleep sounds won't end up as your top artist in December."
      - q: "Can I exclude an artist or album from my Spotify taste profile?"
        a: "No. Spotify only lets you exclude playlists or individual tracks, not artists or albums. Songs you play from somewhere else, such as Liked Songs, still count. To undo an exclusion, open the same menu and choose the Include option."
