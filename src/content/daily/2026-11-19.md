    heading: "Myth: AI is killing Indian IT jobs"
    body: >-
      The story going around is that AI is wiping out jobs at Indian IT companies. The real number is less dramatic and more interesting. TCS, Infosys, HCLTech, Wipro and Tech Mahindra had a combined net headcount decline of 7,389 in FY26, their first net drop in years, against a net addition of 12,718 the year before. TCS drove most of it, cutting around 12,000 roles. Here's what doesn't fit the doom story: TCS is still hiring roughly 25,000 freshers this year, and Infosys another 20,000, both down from the 40,000-plus they used to take in, but nowhere close to zero. AI isn't deleting the industry. It's thinning the entry-level pyramid and making companies pickier about who gets to stand at the bottom of it.
