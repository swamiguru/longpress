      The 'world's first 5GHz mobile chip' headlines are here, and GHz is quietly the most overrated spec in tech. Peak clock speed lasts only seconds before heat forces the chip to throttle down, so sustained performance — not the number on the box — is what you actually feel. What really decides how fast a phone feels: chip architecture (how much work it does per cycle, or IPC), RAM management, storage speed, and how well the software is optimized. A well-tuned 3GHz phone beats a poorly-cooled 5GHz one every time. Stop buying the number.

    breakthrough: >-

    catch: >-
