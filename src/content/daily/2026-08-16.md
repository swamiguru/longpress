    heading: "Prompt of the day: the 'Teach-Back' trick"
    body: >-
      Stop asking AI to 'explain' things and flip it instead. Paste this into ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude: 'I'll explain [topic] in my own words. Catch every mistake, gap or fuzzy bit, then give me the corrected version in 5 tight bullets. Be blunt, not polite.' It works because you remember what you struggle to explain, not what you passively read. The prompt forces the struggle, then fixes you instantly. Try it on something you only kind of understand.
    problem: >-
      Using AI to 'explain X' feels productive but is passive; you nod along, close the tab, and retain almost nothing.
    breakthrough: >-
      Flip the roles: you explain the topic in your own words and make the AI act as a strict checker that flags every error and gap, then returns a corrected 5-bullet version. It's the Feynman technique with an instant fact-checker.
    catch: >-
      The AI can still be confidently wrong, so for high-stakes topics verify its corrections against a primary source rather than trusting them outright.
    forYou: >-
      Pick one thing you half-understand today, run the teach-back prompt, and you'll expose your blind spots in about two minutes.
