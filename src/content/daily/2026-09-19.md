    catch: >-
      It works because the page looks like a normal anti-bot check, and the malware runs the moment you paste and hit enter. Antivirus can't help if you launched it yourself.
    forYou: >-
      Memorise one rule: no real website ever needs you to copy-paste a command into Run, Terminal or PowerShell. If a page asks, it's an attack — close the tab.
