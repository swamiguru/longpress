Then quiz me with 3 questions, one at a time — wait for my answer and correct me before the next.” It works in ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude, turning a know-it-all bot into a patient tutor that makes the concept stick. 🧠
    problem: >-
      AI is great at spitting out answers, but reading an answer isn't the same as understanding it — so people 'learn' things they forget an hour later.
    breakthrough: >-
      A simple teach-back prompt makes the model teach instead of tell: explain simply with one analogy, then quiz you one question at a time and correct you before moving on. That active-recall-plus-instant-feedback loop is what actually builds retention.
    catch: >-
      It only works if you answer honestly before peeking — treat it like a real quiz, not a reading exercise, or you get the illusion of learning without the retention.
    forYou: >-
      Save the prompt, swap in whatever you're trying to learn, and use it the next time you'd normally just ask AI to explain something.
