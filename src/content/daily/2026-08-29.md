    body: >-

It reads every minute for 10 days and can flag rising ketones before they turn into a diabetic emergency. Cleared for anyone aged 2 and up living with diabetes, it is a sign of where wearables are heading: minute-by-minute health data your smartwatch still can't measure.
    problem: >-
      Continuous glucose monitors track blood sugar, but ketones, a separate warning sign that can spiral into life-threatening diabetic ketoacidosis (DKA), usually needed separate finger-prick or blood tests, often caught too late.
    breakthrough: >-
      On Aug 25, 2026, the FDA authorized Abbott's Libre Duo 10 Day, the first US wearable to continuously monitor ketones and the world's first to track both glucose and ketones from one skin sensor. It reads every minute for 10 days and can flag rising ketones before DKA sets in. It was cleared for people aged 2+ with diabetes via the FDA's De Novo pathway.
    catch: >-
      This is a medical device for people with diabetes, not a lifestyle gadget. It is US-only for now, tied to a prescription, and priced as a medical consumable rather than a smartwatch feature.
    forYou: >-
      If you or family manage diabetes, a single dual sugar-and-ketone sensor could replace extra testing and catch danger earlier. For everyone else, it is a preview of the real-time health monitoring that mainstream smartwatches are still chasing.
