    body: >-
      The belief that only your phone brand's original charger is safe, and any third-party one will kill your battery, is wrong. Your phone negotiates its own charging speed — a certified third-party USB-C PD charger that supports your phone's protocol is completely safe. The real danger isn't 'third-party,' it's cheap, uncertified bricks with no safety circuits and fake wattage claims. Buy certified and you're fine.
    problem: >-
      Charger fear costs people money and convenience — they either overpay for first-party bricks or, worse, grab the cheapest ₹99 no-name charger, which is where the actual risk lives.
    breakthrough: >-
      Modern phones control their own charging: the handset and a USB Power Delivery charger negotiate voltage and current, so any properly certified third-party charger supporting your phone's protocol charges safely at full speed.
    catch: >-
      'Third-party' isn't the problem — uncertified is. Cheap chargers with missing safety circuits, poor thermal design, and inflated wattage claims can genuinely damage a battery or worse, and heat remains the real long-term enemy regardless of brand.
    forYou: >-
      Buy USB-C PD chargers from reputable brands with proper certification (and the right wattage/protocol for your phone), skip the unbranded mystery bricks, and stop paying a premium just for a logo.
