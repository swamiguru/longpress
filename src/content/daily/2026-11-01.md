    forYou: >-

    heading: "Pull your free credit report once a year"
    body: >-
      India's credit bureaus are required by an RBI directive, effective January 1, 2017, to give you one free full credit report per calendar year, score included, and almost nobody uses it. After a leak claim like the AU Small Finance Bank one, this is the cheapest check there is: open each bureau's site, verify yourself, and read every loan and card line. If you don't recognise one, that's your answer.
    problem: >-
      Leaked Aadhaar and PAN details are what fraudsters use to take loans in someone else's name, and you usually find out when a recovery call arrives months later.
    breakthrough: >-
      The [RBI circular](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_CircularIndexDisplay.aspx?Id=10590) requires each credit information company to provide a free full credit report once a year, at any time of your choosing, in electronic form, with the same detail lenders get. Each bureau has to publish the access procedure on its own website. Use the official site of each one rather than a third-party app.
    catch: >-
      It is one free report per bureau per year, so it is a periodic audit and not live monitoring. Menu names differ from bureau to bureau and change often.
    forYou: >-
      Pick a date, maybe right after any breach news, and pull all the bureaus. If an account looks unfamiliar, raise it with the lender and file a dispute with the bureau.
    question: "How do I get a free credit report in India and what should I look for?"
    answer: >-
      Under an RBI directive effective January 1, 2017, every credit information company in India must give you one free full credit report per calendar year, including your score. Request it from each bureau's own website after identity checks, then look for loans or cards you did not open.
    qa:
      - q: "How often can I get a free credit report from CIBIL and other bureaus in India?"
        a: "Once per calendar year from each credit information company, chosen at any point in the year. The RBI circular describes the access as once during a year and says the report must carry the same detail lenders see, including your score, delivered electronically."
      - q: "Who has to give me a free credit report in India?"
        a: "Each credit information company that holds your credit data, which covers India's licensed bureaus such as CIBIL, Experian, Equifax and CRIF High Mark. Each must publish the access procedure on its own website. Because they hold separate records, check all of them, not just one."

    heading: "SIM binding will hit honest users before scammers"
    body: >-
      The Department of Telecommunications has told WhatsApp, Telegram, Signal and other messaging apps to stay bound to the registered SIM, and reports point to January 1, 2027 as the start after the deadline slipped from early 2026. The intent is to cut impersonation and fraud, which is a fair goal. My issue is who pays: the traveller, the tablet user and the person with a dual-SIM phone, while a scammer who already holds SIMs doesn't blink.
    problem: >-
      Scam networks run on WhatsApp and Telegram accounts that outlive the SIM they were registered with, so DoT wants the account tied to a SIM that is physically present and active in the phone.
    breakthrough: >-
      The directive came in November 2025 under the Telecommunication Cyber Security Amendment Rules, and [The Mainstream](https://themainstream.co.in/?p=119491) reported in April 2026 that the deadline moved to the end of 2026. [MediaNama](https://www.medianama.com/2026/10/223-event-announcement-technical-issues-with-sim-binding-online-november-13-2026-nama/) says it is expected to be operational from January 1, 2027 and is holding an invite-only roundtable on November 13.
    catch: >-
      Nothing is final: the start date comes from media reports, WhatsApp's SIM binding support hasn't reached the public app, and it isn't confirmed how Apple's iOS and Google's Android will expose SIM status to apps.
    forYou: >-
      If you use WhatsApp on a Wi-Fi-only tablet, a second phone or while roaming, expect friction when this lands. The fix people want is a narrow rule, aimed at fraud signals, instead of one that punishes everyone with a spare device.
    question: "What is SIM binding for WhatsApp in India and when does it start?"
    answer: >-
      SIM binding is a Department of Telecommunications rule that messaging apps like WhatsApp and Telegram must stay tied to the SIM used to register. Reports point to a start on January 1, 2027, after the deadline slipped to the end of 2026. Apps would stop working without the active SIM in the phone.
    qa:
      - q: "Which apps does India's SIM binding rule cover?"
        a: "The DoT directive covers messaging platforms such as WhatsApp, Telegram, Signal, Snapchat and Messenger. Signal, Telegram and WhatsApp have raised implementation concerns, while Apple and Google asked for more time, with Apple citing technical limits in iOS. Meta is reportedly talking to the DoT about a workable approach."
      - q: "How will SIM binding affect Wi-Fi-only devices and travellers in India?"
        a: "Experts quoted by MediaNama warn of disruption on Wi-Fi-only devices, multi-device setups, travel and emergencies, because the apps would need repeated re-authentication. eSIMs and dual-SIM phones raise further implementation questions, and Airtel and Vodafone Idea's silent authentication would not confirm the SIM is physically present."
