    body: >-
      The old advice to fully drain your phone to 0% and charge back to 100% comes from nickel batteries that died years ago. Modern phones use lithium-ion, and deep 0-100 cycles plus heat are exactly what age them fastest. The healthier habit is boring: keep it roughly between 20% and 80% for daily use, top up in small bites whenever, and avoid charging in a hot car or under a pillow. Charging to 100% occasionally is fine — living at the extremes and cooking the battery is what quietly kills capacity. Most phones now have an '80% limit' or adaptive charging toggle; turn it on and stop babysitting the percentage.
    problem: >-
      A ton of people still 'condition' their phone by draining it to 0% and charging to 100%, believing it's healthy — advice inherited from old nickel-based batteries.
    breakthrough: >-
      Modern phones use lithium-ion, where deep 0-100 cycles and heat are the biggest capacity killers. Keeping the charge roughly between 20% and 80%, topping up in small amounts, and avoiding hot environments preserves battery health far better. Most phones now include an 80% charge limit or adaptive charging that automates this.
    catch: >-
      The odd full charge before a long day is totally fine — this is about not living at the extremes, not paranoia. And battery wear is gradual, so you won't 'feel' the damage from bad habits until a year or two in.
    forYou: >-
      Turn on your phone's 80% / optimized charging setting, keep it off hot surfaces while charging, and stop deliberately running it to 0% — your two-year-old battery will thank you.
