    body: >-
      A Windows feature most people never turn on: press Win + V instead of Ctrl + V and you get clipboard history — a list of your last 25 copied items. You can paste any older copy (not just the most recent), pin the ones you reuse like addresses, code snippets or links, and sync the list across your PCs. Enable it once under Settings > System > Clipboard.
    problem: >-
      The default clipboard holds exactly one thing. Copy something new and the previous item is gone — so any multi-step copy-paste job means bouncing back and forth between windows.
    breakthrough: >-
      Windows has built-in clipboard history behind Win + V: up to 25 recent copies, pinning for items you reuse, and optional cross-device sync — all native, no extra app to install.
    catch: >-
      It's off by default and you have to enable it once. Cross-PC sync needs a Microsoft account sign-in, and the history clears on restart unless an item is pinned.
    forYou: >-
      Turn it on today (Settings > System > Clipboard), pin the two or three things you paste constantly, and stop re-copying — Win + V pays for itself the first busy afternoon.

      Buy for the phone you actually use: iPhone to AirTag, Galaxy to SmartTag 2, mixed devices or tight budget to JioTag 2. Ignore the brand hype and match the network to your daily driver.
