It matters because banking malware abuses Accessibility permissions to read your screen, grab OTPs and even tap buttons for you — it's the number-one trick behind Android account-drain scams. If you spot an app you don't recognise, especially a random 'cleaner' or 'PDF' tool, remove its access now.
    problem: >-
      Android's Accessibility permission is powerful and invisible in daily use, and banking trojans quietly exploit it to take over accounts.
    breakthrough: >-
      Opening Settings > Accessibility shows every app with screen-reading and tap control; revoking access for anything you didn't knowingly enable shuts down the most common Android account-drain technique.
    catch: >-
      Legitimate accessibility apps also live here, so don't blanket-disable everything — remove only apps you don't recognise or didn't set up on purpose.
