    heading: "Check Your AIS Before You File Your ITR"
    body: >-
      Before filing your ITR, check your Annual Information Statement, or AIS, on the income tax e-filing portal. It's the department's own record of every transaction your bank, employer and broker have already reported against your PAN, covering income, TDS, high-value transactions and investments, and it's what pre-fills your return whether you check it or not. Access it by logging into incometax.gov.in with your PAN, then going to the e-File menu and selecting View Annual Information Statement. If an entry is wrong, duplicated, or belongs to someone else, click the Feedback button next to it and the reporting source has to respond. Starting this about a month before you file gives you time to catch and fix mismatches before they turn into a scrutiny notice.
    image: "/images/daily/2026-11-17/illustration_3.png"
    problem: >-
      Most pre-filled ITR fields come straight from your AIS, so an error reported by your bank or employer becomes your problem the moment you file, often surfacing later as a mismatch notice.
    breakthrough: >-
      The income tax portal's AIS section, under the e-File menu, lets you see that data ahead of time and lodge a Feedback entry, incorrect, duplicate, or not yours, against anything wrong before you file.
    catch: >-
      AIS isn't exhaustive. It can miss items like cash rent, so you still have to report everything you earned even when the statement doesn't show it.
    forYou: >-
      Block out thirty minutes before you file this year's return: open your AIS, scan every entry, and flag anything that doesn't look right before the department does it for you.
    question: "What is AIS on the income tax e-filing portal?"
    answer: >-
      AIS, or Annual Information Statement, is the income tax department's own record of your financial transactions for the year, reported by banks, employers and brokers. Found under incometax.gov.in's e-File menu, it feeds the pre-filled fields on your ITR, so checking it before you file helps you catch mismatches early.

      - q: "How do I fix a wrong entry in my AIS?"
        a: "Next to the incorrect entry, click the Feedback option marked Optional, and choose a reason such as incorrect information, duplicate information, or information belonging to someone else.       - q: "Why should I check my AIS before filing my ITR?"
