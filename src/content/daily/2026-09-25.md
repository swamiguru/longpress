    breakthrough: >-
      A single reusable prompt fixes that: ask any AI to return a one-line TL;DR, five key points, three action items (with owner and deadline if mentioned), and one thing that's missing or unclear, all under 150 words. Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude with your text.
    catch: >-
